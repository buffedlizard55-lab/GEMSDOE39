Data and candidate limitations

  • The official competition data page requires login in this environment. Inputs were restored from owner-controlled mirrors at pinned commits; organizer provenance is not independently authenticated.
  • The local feature raster has 19 tagged bands and the sample grid is 3,730 × 3,292, EPSG:32611, 100 m. These are measurements of the restored mirror, not a direct organizer-authenticated download.
  • H39X-01's exact formula was recorded only after its four-block holdout result. The test is exploratory; its fixed SPRT settings do not retroactively preregister the formula. It returned `continue` and is not approved for a weekly slot.
  • H39Y-01 was preregistered, evaluated once, and rejected at H0 after 5 losses in 5 tiles; no new TIFF/ZIP or weekly slot was produced. Its report is linked above.
  • The H39Y evaluator reached its registered stopping boundary, then encountered a duplicate-key error while assembling its final report. Saved outcomes were verified; no fold fit, tile predictions, or per-tile DTI scoring were rerun. Pooled DTI was re-aggregated from saved TP/FP/FN counts. The irregularity is documented in the report.
  • Exact duplicate comparison included 17 local/retrieved prior TIFFs and found no exact match. It is not an exhaustive scan of all sibling projects or public artifacts.
  • No upload, leaderboard score, or private score is associated with the H39X-01 or H39Y-01 work.
