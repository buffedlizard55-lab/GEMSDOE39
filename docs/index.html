Five novel geological hypotheses (H39 family)

H39-A Cross-gradient tensor multi-physics (mag × gravity × MT) eigen-coherence — parallel edges in three independent physics flag a blind contact.
H39-B PRIMARY Scarp curvature step-over / horsetail splay — multi-scale ridge curvature, slope-break gradient, 1 m LiDAR corroboration, with directional collinearity chains.
H39-C Tilt-derivative analytic-signal edges — normalizes deep weak contacts to match shallow-source dynamic range.
H39-D Magnetic multi-scale "worm" ridges (Archibald et al. 1999) with amplitude-ratio persistence across Gaussian continuation heights.
H39-E Basement-depth × conductivity co-located parallel edge — a permeable fault-bounded geothermal aquifer signature.
