Why the previous best (0.2778) scored what it scored

The artifact is gemsdoe32-h33-h33-2-b2-20261004T220000Z-e5eb6e7e-zeros.tif: 37,654 dots. It is the 40,199-dot h27-4-r1-solo-d2-8 artifact (0.2708) with exactly 2,545 dots deleted — every dot at 1.414–2.000 px from the catalogue. Re-read from disk and asserted by calibrate.verify_nested_pair.

Because TPw + FNw = |G|, the metric collapses to DTI = TPw / (0.2·TPw + 0.2·FPw + 0.8·|G|), and that nested pair identifies |G|:

Answer to “can we beat it?” Reaching 0.3262 (live #1) at the anchor's FP mass needs TPw ≈ 6,489 instead of 5,475 — a +18.6% improvement in hit rate (6.13% → ~7.3%). Budget tuning cannot do it: the priced optimum for the incumbent surface is ~25–28k dots for ~0.283. The only lever is more hits per dot, which is what all seven H40 channels are aimed at.

Also settled analytically and pinned by tests/test_h40.py::test_binary_emission_is_never_worse_than_scaling_down: because 0.8·|G| is a constant additive term, DTI(c·p) rises monotonically in c and the inclusion rule does not depend on p. Binary {0,1} emission at full scale is optimal; submitting soft probabilities is strictly worse.

Read the 0% row with care. Holding the hit rate at the anchor's while raising the budget from 37,654 to 45,962 dots mechanically lifts the model's price to 0.2960, but the corpus says that is not how budgets behave: across 16 scored artifacts the implied hit rate falls roughly as n−0.75, so a larger budget does not hold its average hit rate. At 0% transfer the defensible price is therefore the anchor's own measured 0.2778, not 0.2960. Every row above 0% assumes some real transfer of the measured advantage.

Even at a quarter of the measured advantage the price clears today's live #1 (0.3262). The transfer factor itself (0.451) is estimated from a single live-scored artifact and is the weakest link in the chain — it says the SGMC off-catalogue instrument is about 2.2× harder than the live set, measured by running the live-scored 0.2778 artifact through the same instrument.
