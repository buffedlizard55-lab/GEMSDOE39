When a future candidate is approved: upload steps

The official rules snapshot checked 2026-10-05 allows up to three scoring submissions per week and requires selecting one final submission for prize rounds. Recheck the live rules before upload; a weekly scoring slot is limited feedback, not a harmless file-format test.

  1. Download the specific single-band GeoTIFF linked from the project's home page. Keep its manifest and SHA-256 with it. Do not rename it to a previous candidate's filename.
  2. Open the official DOE GEMS submissions page and sign in to the correct competition account/team.
  3. Choose New submission. In the file picker, select the downloaded .tif (not its `.json` manifest, not a ZIP, not a preview PNG).
  4. Paste the unique short note from that candidate's manifest or approved submission card into the optional description/note box. Keep candidate ID, feature family, and budget so the result is traceable.
  5. Before submission, confirm the gate explicitly says PASS / eligible, the file checksum matches its manifest, and the competition page still permits submissions. If anything is out of date or the gate is not PASS, stop.
  6. Submit once and record the returned score and submission ID. A public leaderboard row does not reveal its TIFF filename, and a public score is not a private leaderboard score.

Do not upload the present H39X-01 candidate under this checklist. It does not currently satisfy step 5.

Why range errors occur—and the correct `[0,1]` treatment

The official competition instructions require a floating-point prediction raster on the specified projected 100 m grid, values between 0 and 1 for cells inside the official bounds, and null/NoData values outside those bounds. In this project, the NaN outside the rasterized official footprint is the null representation. Do not replace outside-footprint NaNs with zero: zero is a prediction, not an out-of-bounds marker. Do not silently clip a broken raster without investigating the generating transform.

  • Check finite in-footprint cells for both bounds: min ≥ 0 and max ≤ 1.
  • Check that every outside-footprint cell is NaN and that there are no infinities.
  • Check one band, Float32, dimensions, pixel size, affine transform, and CRS against the official schema and pinned footprint.
  • Use the local gate: python scripts/validate_submission.py <candidate.tif>. A successful validator is local evidence; it does not guarantee the organizer's upload validator will accept a file.
The downloaded H39X-01 artifact passed the project's 14 local checks: EPSG:32611; 3292 × 3730; 100 m; Float32; 5,167,373 finite in-footprint cells; 7,111,787 outside-footprint NaNs; no infinities. The outside-mask count is a count of pixels, not a competition score.

Evidence and limitations

  • Exploratory holdout: spatial blocks NW/NE/SW/SE gave H39X-01 proxy DTIs 0.010223 / 0.000000 / 0.069819 / 0.060368 and incumbent values 0.029566 / 0.014530 / 0.033305 / 0.022261. The candidate won SW and SE only; pooled descriptive proxy DTI was 0.052944 vs 0.024763.
  • Sequential test: SPRT settings p₀=.50, p₁=.70, α=.05, β=.10; LLR −0.348707; thresholds −2.302585 and 2.995732; decision continue. Whole spatial blocks are correlated, so the independence assumption may be optimistic. Fixed folds are exhausted: no informal peeking, repeated variants, or seed-searching on the same holdout.
  • Multiplicity: the four hypotheses are ranked in the current register, but no contemporaneous record establishes that this ranking preceded H39X-01 development. The exact detector recipe was not time-stamped before scoring. Treat the current result as post-hoc/exploratory, not confirmatory. A future holdout must be newly locked; either select one candidate before opening it or predeclare a family-wise/sequential correction for all candidates tested.
  • Proxy labels: competition labels describe new faults absent from the supplied known-fault set; local proxy labels cannot establish private-set ranking or geothermal productivity.
  • Uniqueness scope: exact comparison covered 17 locally available/retrieved prior TIFFs and found no exact match. It is not an exhaustive global scan.
  • Data provenance: inputs were restored from SHA-256-pinned owner mirrors because the competition data portal is login-gated. Integrity hashes do not prove organizer provenance. The source feed records score-attribution irregularities and official links.
